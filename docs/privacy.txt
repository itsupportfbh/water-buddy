Privacy — Water Buddy desktop

The desktop application stores drink entries, preferences, last reminder time
and mascot position locally in data.json beside the installed app. It also writes
local diagnostic status and may preserve backups when saved data cannot be read.
It does not upload drink history, use analytics, display advertising, or contact
a remote server for reminders. Opening the external hydration reference link
uses your browser and the destination site's privacy practices.

The download website and any future GitHub/SignPath services have their own
privacy policies. Desktop and browser water histories are separate.
Uninstall retains local history; users can remove the remaining WaterBuddy folder
after uninstall if they want to erase it. Do not submit your data.json in issues.
