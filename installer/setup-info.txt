Water Buddy for Windows

Setup installs Water Buddy for your Windows account without administrator access.
Automatic startup is enabled at sign-in. The bottle buddy appears after installation.

Reminders run hourly from 7 AM to 4 PM in your computer's local time.
Your PC must be awake and you must be signed in. Closing the browser is fine.
The app does not wake a sleeping PC or replay missed reminders in a batch.

Already running Water Buddy? Choose Exit from its tray menu before updating.
Updates keep your drink history and preferences. Desktop and web histories are separate.
To avoid duplicate reminders, pause the website's reminders when using the desktop app.

Open Water Buddy from the Start menu or its tray icon.
Disable startup in Preferences. Uninstall from Windows Settings > Apps or
Uninstall Water Buddy in the Start menu. Saved drink history is retained.

This setup file is unsigned. If your device or company policy blocks it, ask
your IT administrator to review it. Do not disable security protections.
